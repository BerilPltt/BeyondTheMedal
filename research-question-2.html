Model Based Visualizations

Research Focus

Predicting whether athletes, categorized by gender, nationality, and discipline, fall into the "Equal" or "Unequal" class is the main goal of the classification task. Important information about the model's performance is provided via the ROC curve, confusion matrix, and assessment metrics. The model's overall accuracy of roughly 79% indicates that it anticipates the right class most of the time. Its performance in the two classes differs considerably, though.

Exploring Gender Parity in Global Sports Participation

The model performs well for the "Unequal" class, correctly identifying 1,380 instances (true positives) and incorrectly classifying only 158 instances as "Equal" (false positives). This results in a precision of 90%, meaning that when the model predicts "Unequal," it is correct 90% of the time. The recall for this class is 82%, demonstrating that the model correctly catches a high proportion of the actual "Unequal" instances. These metrics illustrate the model's performance in detecting the "Unequal" category. On the other hand, the model has more difficulties with the "Equal" class. With a precision of 71% and a noticeably lower recall of 56%, it properly classifies 382 cases as "Equal" (true negatives) but incorrectly classifies 302 examples as "Unequal" (false negatives). This suggests that although the model has a moderate level of accuracy in predicting "Equal," it frequently misses real "Equal" instances, most likely as a result of class imbalance or less clear characteristics dividing the two groups.
